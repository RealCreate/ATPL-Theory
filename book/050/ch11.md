## Appendix · Formula Sheet and Glossary

### Formulas and rules of thumb

> **Temperature**
>
> | Rule | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | ISA temperature below 36 090 ft | 15 − 2 × (altitude in 1000 ft) °C (exact factor 1.98) |
> | ISA temperature from 36 090 ft to 65 600 ft | −56.5 °C |
> | ISA deviation | Actual temperature − ISA temperature at that flight level |
> | Conversions | °F = 9/5 × °C + 32 · K = °C + 273.15 · −40 °C = −40 °F |
> | Freezing level (rough) | Surface temperature ÷ 2, in 1000 ft above ground |

> **Lapse rates**
>
> | Rate | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | ISA (mean ELR) | 0.65 °C/100 m = 1.98 ≈ 2 °C/1000 ft |
> | DALR | 1 °C/100 m = 3 °C/1000 ft |
> | SALR (exam value, low levels) | 0.6 °C/100 m = 1.8 °C/1000 ft |
> | Dew point in rising unsaturated air | Falls ≈ 0.2 °C/100 m |
> | Spread closing in rising air | ≈ 0.8 °C/100 m = 2.5 °C/1000 ft |
> | Convective cloud base | Spread × 400 ft (× 125 m) |
> | Föhn gain | (3 − 1.8) × thickness of cloud climbed (1000 ft) °C |
> | Stability | ELR < SALR stable · SALR < ELR < DALR conditionally unstable · ELR > DALR absolutely unstable |

> **Pressure and altimetry**
>
> | Rule | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | ISA MSL | 1013.25 hPa = 29.92 inHg = 760 mmHg; 1.225 kg/m³; +15 °C |
> | Barometric lapse rate near MSL | 30 ft (9 m) per hPa |
> | Barometric lapse rate aloft | ≈ 50 ft/hPa at 18 000 ft · ≈ 70 ft/hPa at FL 300 · ≈ 100 ft/hPa at FL 400 |
> | QNH from QFE | QNH = QFE + elevation ÷ 30 (ft → hPa) |
> | Pressure altitude | Altitude + (1013 − QNH) × 30 ft |
> | True height at a flight level (ISA temperature) | FL × 100 − (1013 − QNH) × 30 ft |
> | Temperature correction | 4 % per 10 °C ISA deviation = 4 ft per 1000 ft per °C, from the setting datum |
> | Half pressure / half density / quarter density | 18 000 ft / 22 000 ft / 40 000 ft |
> | QFF vs QNH (station above MSL) | Colder than ISA: QFF > QNH · warmer: QFF < QNH |
> | Rule of thumb | From high to low (pressure or temperature), look out below |
> | Equation of state | P = ρ R T (R = 287 J/kg·K, T in kelvin) |

> **Wind**
>
> | Rule | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | Coriolis | 2 Ω V sin φ: right in NH, zero at equator |
> | Geostrophic wind | Vg = (ΔP/Δn) ÷ (ρ × 2Ω sin φ): stronger at lower latitude |
> | Gradient wind | Round a high > straight > round a low |
> | Surface vs 2000 ft wind | Land: 50 %, backed 30° · Sea: 70 %, backed 10° (NH; veered in SH) |
> | Buys Ballot | Back to the wind, low on the left (NH) |
> | Jet stream | ≥ 60 kt (definition); ≥ 80 kt drawn on SWC |
> | CAT thresholds | Vertical shear ≥ 5 kt/1000 ft; horizontal ≥ 20 kt/100 nm |
> | Gust in METAR | Peak exceeds mean by ≥ 10 kt |

> **Fronts and storms**
>
> | Item | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | Warm front slope | ≈ 1:150 (1:100 to 1:200); speed ≈ 2/3 of normal geostrophic component |
> | Cold front slope | ≈ 1:80 (1:50 to 1:100); speed ≈ full normal component |
> | Cold front vs warm front speed | ≈ 1.5 × |
> | Microburst | ≤ 4 km (2.2 nm); peak 2–5 min; life ≈ 10–15 min |
> | TRS | ≥ 64 kt; SST ≥ 26–27 °C; latitude ≥ 5° |
> | Lightning distance | Seconds ÷ 3 = km |
> | Cb avoidance | ≥ 10 nm (20 nm severe or high level); overfly by ≥ 5000 ft |
> | Tropical freezing level | ≈ 15 000–16 000 ft |

> **Observations and codes**
>
> | Item | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | Fog / mist / haze | < 1000 m / 1000–5000 m wet / dry particles |
> | Oktas | FEW 1–2 · SCT 3–4 · BKN 5–7 · OVC 8 |
> | Ceiling | Lowest BKN or OVC below 20 000 ft |
> | METAR wind / tower wind | 10 min, true / 2 min, magnetic |
> | RVR reported | When visibility or RVR < 1500 m |
> | TEMPO | Each < 1 h, total < half the period |
> | SIGMET validity | ≤ 4 h (6 h for VA and TC) |
> | Pressure-level heights | 850 ≈ FL 050 · 700 ≈ FL 100 · 500 ≈ FL 180 · 300 ≈ FL 300 · 250 ≈ FL 340 · 200 ≈ FL 390 |
